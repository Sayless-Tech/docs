Companies represent accounts or organizations. Use them when post-sales customer work needs a shared account record instead of living only inside conversations and tickets.

## Overview

A company page brings together the account context your team needs before replying, investigating, or reviewing customer activity.

Companies can include overview details, conversations, tickets, contacts, peers, metrics, files, notes, and pricing records.

Company records are also used by channel integrations. Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Microsoft Teams channel mappings can attach group or channel conversations to the right company.

Go to [Companies](https://joinsayless.com/companies).

| Company tab | Route pattern | What it shows |

## Work with companies

Use the company list for account management.

### Create a company

Create a company from the company list or command menu when you need a new account record.

Company creation can be limited by plan or permissions. If creation is unavailable, check billing limits and your role.

> The Free plan includes up to `150` companies. Startup, Business, and Enterprise are unlimited in the current client policy. Admins can review [Settings \> Billing](https://joinsayless.com/settings/billing) when creation is unavailable.

### Edit a company

Edit company details when the name, owner, labels, sector, employee count, website, or other attributes change.

Company edits open in a guide-style panel so you can update details without losing the surrounding list or company context.

### Delete a company

Delete a company only when the record should be removed. Bulk delete actions may be available from the company list.

Deleting a company can remove the account-level place where related conversations, tickets, files, notes, and metrics are organized. Use labels or ownership changes when the company should remain available but be categorized differently.

## Company details

Open a company to review account-level context.

### Overview

The overview summarizes key company details and related activity.

Use it before replying to a conversation from that account. It gives faster context than jumping across separate lists.

### Conversations

Company conversations show message threads linked to the company.

You can open a conversation detail from the company page and navigate back to the company context.

### Tickets

Company tickets show follow-up work linked to the company.

The company ticket list supports filtering, sorting, pagination, row menus, and deletion actions.

### Contacts

Company contacts show people associated with the account.

### Peers

Peers connect related companies.

Use peers for parent-child accounts, partner relationships, or companies that should be reviewed together.

### Metrics

Company metrics show event or product activity connected to the account.

## Filtering and views

Use the company list to manage many records.

### Search

Search helps find companies by name and visible fields.

### Filters and columns

Filters and columns help compare owner, labels, sector, size, updated time, and other company fields.

The company table includes fields for profile, labels, size, funding, tags, updated time, and other text fields where available.

### Bulk actions

Select multiple companies when you need to apply list-level actions such as deletion.
