Company properties make account records searchable, filterable, and useful across conversations, tickets, and reports.

## Overview

Keep company properties current so teammates can quickly understand the account and route work correctly.

## Company properties

Company properties appear in company forms, lists, detail pages, and filters.

### Name

The company name is the primary identifier for the account.

### Owner

Owner indicates who is responsible for the company relationship or account context.

### Labels

Labels categorize companies by segment, state, priority, or internal grouping.

### Sector

Sector describes the company’s industry or market.

### Employee count

Employee count helps classify company size.

### Website

Website and domains help identify and enrich the company record.

## Relationships

Relationships connect the company to the rest of the workspace.

### Contacts

Contacts are the people associated with the company.

### Conversations

Linked conversations show communication history for the account.

### Tickets

Linked tickets show open and historical follow-up work.

### Peers

Peers connect related companies.
