Pricing records help your team keep commercial context attached to a company.

## Overview

Use the Pricing tab when you need to track pricing details for a company, attach related contracts, or review pricing history.

## Pricing records

Pricing records are managed from the company Pricing tab.

### Create pricing

Create a pricing record when an account needs pricing information stored in Sayless.

### Edit pricing

Edit a pricing record when terms, values, expiry dates, or contract information changes.

### Attach contracts

Attach contract files or links when the pricing record needs source documentation.

### Delete pricing

Delete pricing records only when the information should be removed from the company.

## Pricing history

Pricing history helps teams understand how pricing changed over time.

### Created by

Review who created the pricing record.

### Updated values

Use history to understand what changed and when.

### Contract attachments

Open related contract attachments when reviewing pricing context.
