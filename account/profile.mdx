Your profile identifies you across the workspace. It appears in places like assignment fields, member lists, comments, activity entries, and ownership metadata.

## Overview

Use profile settings to keep your name, avatar, and account information accurate. This helps teammates recognize who owns work and who made changes.

Go to [Settings \> Profile](https://joinsayless.com/{workspace}/settings/profile).

| Field | Where it appears | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Assignments, member lists, activity entries | Helps teammates identify ownership and changes. |
| Avatar | Assignee fields, comments, presence, member lists | Makes dense workflows easier to scan. |
| Email | Account identity and workspace membership | Connects your login to the workspace. |

## Update your profile

Open settings and go to **Profile**.

### Name

Use the name your teammates will recognize. Your name can appear in assignee menus, member tables, activity history, and created-by fields.

### Avatar

Your avatar helps identify you in dense areas of the app, including assignee fields, comments, active user indicators, and member lists.

### Email address

Your email identifies your account and is used for login and workspace membership. If your organization uses invite or domain-based access, keep this aligned with your work email.

> If your email does not match your organization’s allowed domains, you may need a workspace admin to update access settings or send a new invite.

## Workspace profile

Your account can belong to one or more workspaces. Each workspace can show your membership, role, and access independently.

### Role

Your role determines what you can manage. Admins can configure workspace-level settings such as billing, members, integrations, API keys, and webhooks.

### Membership

If you are part of multiple workspaces, make sure you are working in the correct one before changing settings, replying to conversations, or creating records.
