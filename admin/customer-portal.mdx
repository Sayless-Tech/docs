---
title: Customer portal
description: Let customers sign in, open tickets, and submit forms for one workspace.
---

The customer portal is a site for that workspace’s customers. Open [Settings → Customer Portal](https://joinsayless.com/{workspace}/settings/customer-portal).

Turn **Enable portal** on before customers can sign in. Other settings stay saved while it is off.

From the portal settings you can:

- Preview the portal as a read-only admin for a company.
- Choose which ticket fields customers see, and the customer-facing labels for ticket statuses.
- Set branding, header links, and footer links.
- Define portal roles for customer users.
- Attach a custom domain.

Tickets reach the portal when their visibility allows it. A [trigger](/admin/triggers) can set portal visibility or send a [ticket form](/admin/ticket-forms). Submitting a form creates a ticket.

The portal is included from Growth upward. Core workspaces do not have it.
