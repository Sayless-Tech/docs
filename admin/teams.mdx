---
title: Teams
description: Group workspace members into teams and use those teams on conversations and tickets.
---

Teams are groups of members inside a workspace. Use them when ownership should sit with a group rather than one person: a region, a pod, or an on-call rotation.

## Manage teams

Admins manage teams in [Settings → Teams](https://joinsayless.com/{workspace}/settings/teams).

- Add or remove members, and set each member’s role on the team.
- Place a team under a parent team when the org has pods inside a larger group.
- Retire a team when it should stop receiving new work. Existing conversations and tickets keep their history.

You only see teams you belong to when you assign work.

## Assign a team

A team and an assignee are different fields. The assignee is one person. The team is the group that owns the work with them.

On a conversation, open the thread and use **Team** in the details panel. Pick a team, or clear it to remove the team. Clearing the team does not unassign the person.

On a ticket, set **Team** from the ticket form or the details panel. The ticket list can show a Team column. Filters can narrow the list to one team.

Assignment changes show up in the activity history on the conversation or ticket.

A [trigger](/admin/triggers) can set the team automatically, for example when a conversation is created.
