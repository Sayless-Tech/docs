---
title: Teams
description: Group workspace members into teams and use those teams on conversations and tickets.
---

Teams are groups of members inside a workspace. Use them when ownership should sit with a group rather than one person: a region, a pod, or an on-call rotation.

Admins manage teams in [Settings → Teams](https://app.joinsayless.com). Members can be added, removed, and given a role on the team. A team can sit under a parent team.

From a conversation or ticket you can add or remove a team the same way you assign a person. The public API exposes the same membership under Teams in the API reference.
