# Mintlify navigation note

Navigation is configured in `docs.json` (Developers tab). Keep that tab in sync with new pages under `developers/`.

Core pages:

- `developers/overview`
- `developers/quickstart`
- `developers/authentication`
- `developers/api/*`
- `developers/webhooks`
- `developers/sdk/*`
- `developers/changelog`

The product home (`index.mdx`) links a Developers card to `/developers/overview`.
