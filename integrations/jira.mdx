---
title: "Jira integration"
description: "Create or link Jira issues from Sayless tickets and follow their progress without leaving the ticket."
---

When a customer problem needs work from engineering, product, or operations, the Jira integration lets you raise it in Jira straight from the Sayless ticket, or link an issue that already exists. Changes to the issue in Jira show up on the ticket's timeline.

Available on all plans. Each agent connects their own Jira account; issues are created as that person.

## How it works

- **Create issue** opens a form on the ticket and creates the issue in Jira.
- **Link issue** attaches an existing Jira issue to the ticket. You can search, or pick from recent issues.
- **Linked issues** are listed on the ticket with a link to open each one in Jira.
- **Updates from Jira** are added to the ticket's timeline: changes to the issue's **status**, **priority**, and **assignee**.

A ticket can have several linked issues, and one issue can be linked to several tickets. Linking doesn't change the ticket itself.

## Connect Jira

1. Open [Settings \> Apps](https://joinsayless.com/settings/apps).
2. Find **Jira** and click **Add**, then approve access in Atlassian and choose your Jira site.

Each teammate who wants to create or link issues connects their own account. The ticket's Jira section shows **No Jira account connected** until you do.

## Create an issue from a ticket

1. Open the ticket and find **Jira** in the details panel.
2. Click **Create issue**.
3. Choose the **Project** and **Issue type**, then enter a **Title** and **Description**.
4. Optionally place it on a **Board** (with a **Sprint** and **Board column**) or give it a starting **Status**.
5. Create the issue. It's linked to the ticket right away.

## Link an existing issue

1. In the ticket's **Jira** section, click **Link issue**.
2. Search by summary or key (for example `SUP-42`), or choose a recent issue.

## What you'll see on the ticket

When the issue changes in Jira, the ticket timeline gets an entry such as *SUP-42 status updated to Done* or *assignee updated to Sam Lee*.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No Jira section actions on a ticket | Connect your Jira account in **Settings \> Apps**. |
| A project or issue type is missing | Your Jira account needs permission to create issues in that project. |
| Jira changes don't show on the ticket | Only status, priority, and assignee changes are tracked. |
