---
title: "Email integration"
description: "Forward your support address into the Sayless inbox, reply from Sayless, and send from your own domain."
---

Email turns messages sent to your shared address (for example `support@yourcompany.com`) into Sayless conversations. Your team replies from Sayless, and the customer's answers come back into the same conversation.

Available on all plans. An admin sets it up in two parts: **forwarding** (required, brings email in) and **domain authentication** (recommended, sends replies from your own domain).

## How it works

- **Forwarding:** Sayless gives you a private forwarding address. Your mailbox forwards incoming email to it, and each new email becomes a conversation.
- **Threading:** replies are matched to their conversation by the email's reply headers, so a customer's answer joins the existing thread instead of starting a new one.
- **Replies:** your replies are sent with a **Reply-To** of your forwarding address, so the customer's next answer always comes back to Sayless.
- **Contacts:** the sender becomes a contact. A work email domain also links them to a company.

### Who replies come from

| Domain authentication | Replies are sent from |
| --- | --- |
| Not set up | *Agent Name* `<YourWorkspace@joinsayless.com>` |
| Verified | The agent's own address, for example *Agent Name* `<agent@yourcompany.com>` |

Set up domain authentication so customers see your company's address and your emails are less likely to land in spam.

### What's filtered out

- **Promotional and bulk email** (newsletters, marketing with unsubscribe links) isn't turned into conversations.
- **Blocked senders and domains** are ignored. Manage them in [Settings \> Spam](https://joinsayless.com/settings/spam).

## Set up email forwarding

1. Open [Settings \> Email](https://joinsayless.com/settings/email).
2. Under **Email Forwarding**, enter your support address (for example `support@yourcompany.com`) and save.
3. Copy the **Forward Address** Sayless shows. It looks like `…@parse.joinsayless.com`.
4. In your email provider, forward all incoming mail for that address to the forward address:
   - **Google Workspace / Gmail:** in the mailbox's **Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP**, add the forward address and choose **Forward a copy of incoming mail**. For a Google Group, add the forward address as a member instead.
   - **Microsoft 365 / Outlook:** add a mailbox rule (or an admin mail-flow rule) that forwards or redirects all messages to the forward address. Your admin may need to allow external forwarding.
5. Send a test email to your support address and check that it appears in the inbox.

Gmail sends a confirmation code when you add a forwarding address. It arrives in Sayless as a conversation; copy the code from there.

## Set up domain authentication

1. In [Settings \> Email](https://joinsayless.com/settings/email), click **Setup Domain Authentication** and enter your domain (for example `yourcompany.com`).
2. Open the domain to see the DNS records Sayless needs.
3. Add each record at your DNS provider exactly as shown.
4. Come back to the domain page and check its status. DNS changes can take from a few minutes to 48 hours to apply.

Once the domain is verified, agents whose Sayless email is on that domain send replies from their own address.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Emails don't arrive | Forwarding is on in your mail provider and points at the exact forward address. External forwarding is allowed (Microsoft 365 blocks it by default). |
| A newsletter or automated email didn't arrive | Promotional and bulk email is filtered out on purpose. |
| A sender's emails don't arrive | The sender or their domain isn't blocked in **Settings \> Spam**. |
| A customer reply started a new conversation | Their mail client dropped the reply headers, or they wrote a fresh email instead of replying. |
| Replies come from `@joinsayless.com` | Domain authentication isn't verified yet, or the agent's Sayless email isn't on that domain. |
| Domain stays unverified | Records match exactly (no extra domain suffix added by your DNS provider), then allow time to propagate before changing them. |

## Remove email

Removing email forwarding stops new emails from syncing. Conversations already in Sayless stay. Also turn off forwarding in your mail provider.
