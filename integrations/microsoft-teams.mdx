---
title: "Microsoft Teams integration"
description: "Bring Microsoft Teams channel threads with customers into the Sayless inbox and reply from Sayless."
---

If your customers or partners work with you in Microsoft Teams channels, the Teams integration brings those channels into the Sayless inbox. Each Teams post and its replies become one conversation your team can assign, track, and answer. Replies go back to the thread in Teams.

Available on the **Enterprise** plan. Setup needs a Microsoft Teams admin and a short step with Sayless support, because the Sayless Teams app is installed per organization.

## How it works

- **The workspace connection** links Sayless to your Microsoft 365 tenant through an admin account.
- **The Sayless Teams app** is added to each Team channel you want in Sayless. It reads new posts and replies, and posts your replies.
- **Each Teams thread is one conversation.** A new post starts a conversation; replies to it land in the same one.
- **Personal connections** let each agent reply as themselves.

### What syncs

| From Teams to Sayless | From Sayless to Teams |
| --- | --- |
| New posts and replies, with attachments | Your replies, with attachments |
| Edits (the message is refreshed in full) | Your replies, as yourself when connected |
| Reactions | |
| Mentions | Mentions |

When someone new writes, Sayless creates a contact from their Teams account; a work email links them to that company. Link each channel to its company so its conversations show on the company's page.

## Set up Microsoft Teams

You need to be a Sayless workspace admin and have a Microsoft Teams admin account. Teams also needs to be turned on for your workspace; if you don't see **Microsoft Teams** in settings, contact [support@joinsayless.com](mailto:support@joinsayless.com).

1. Open [Settings \> Microsoft Teams](https://joinsayless.com/settings/teams) and click **Connect**. Sign in with your Microsoft Teams admin account and approve the permissions.
2. Click **Email support for the Teams app**. Sayless support sends you the Sayless Teams app for your organization.
3. In Teams, install the app, then add it to each Team channel you want to sync.
4. Back in Sayless, open [Settings \> Microsoft Teams \> Channels](https://joinsayless.com/settings/teams/channels) and link each channel to its company.

Posts made after the app joins a channel appear in the inbox.

### Reply as yourself (each agent, optional)

Open [Settings \> Apps](https://joinsayless.com/settings/apps) and add **Microsoft Teams** with your own account. Your replies then come from you. Your account must be in the same Microsoft tenant as the admin connection; otherwise Sayless keeps sending as the app.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Microsoft Teams isn't in settings | Teams needs the Enterprise plan and has to be turned on for your workspace by Sayless support. |
| A channel doesn't appear | The Sayless Teams app has been added to that channel in Teams. |
| Messages don't show on a company | Link the channel to the company in **Settings \> Microsoft Teams \> Channels**. |
| Replies come from the app, not you | Connect your personal Teams account in **Settings \> Apps**, from the same tenant. |
| "Microsoft Teams temporarily unavailable" | Try again shortly; if it persists, contact support. |

## Disconnect

Disconnecting Microsoft Teams stops it for the whole workspace: new posts stop coming in and replies can't be sent. Conversations already in Sayless stay.
