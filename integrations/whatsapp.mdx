---
title: "WhatsApp integration"
description: "WhatsApp isn't available on current Sayless plans."
---

WhatsApp isn't available on current Sayless plans, and it no longer appears in settings. Conversations already brought in from WhatsApp stay in your inbox.

If your team works with customers on WhatsApp, contact [support@joinsayless.com](mailto:support@joinsayless.com).

For group-based customer channels available today, see [Slack](/integrations/slack), [Telegram](/integrations/telegram), and [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams).
