---

Broadcasts can be edited or managed depending on their state. Drafts are usually editable. Sent or canceled broadcasts are more limited.

## Overview

Open a broadcast from the list to review its content, metadata, engagement, and available actions.

Go to [Broadcasts](https://joinsayless.com/{workspace}/broadcasts).

| Broadcast state | Usually available | Usually restricted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Edit, delete, send | Engagement review |
| Sent | View, duplicate | Editing original content |
| Canceled | View, delete where allowed | Sending |

## Edit a broadcast

Use the edit flow when a broadcast has not been finalized.

### Open a draft

Open the broadcast and choose the edit action when it is available.

### Update content

Change the message in the broadcast editor.

### Change destination

Update the destination or sender profile when the broadcast setup allows it.

## Manage broadcasts

Management actions appear in menus from the list or detail page.

### Duplicate broadcast

Duplicate a broadcast when you want to reuse its structure or content for a new message.

### Cancel broadcast

Cancel a broadcast when it should not be sent.

### Delete broadcast

Delete broadcasts only when they should be removed from the workspace.
