Contacts represent people your team communicates with or tracks as part of post-sales customer work.

## Overview

Use contacts when you need person-level context: name, email, phone, company, job title, conversations, and tickets.

Go to [Contacts](https://joinsayless.com/contacts).

> The Free plan includes up to `500` contacts. Startup, Business, and Enterprise are unlimited in the current client policy.

| Contact tab | Route pattern | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | `/contacts/{contactPath}` | Person details and summary. |
| Conversations | `/contacts/{contactPath}/conversations` | Linked conversations. |
| Tickets | `/contacts/{contactPath}/tickets` | Linked follow-up work. |

## Work with contacts

Use the contact list to manage people across the workspace.

### Create a contact

Create a contact when a person needs a durable record in Sayless.

### Edit a contact

Edit a contact when their details, company, email, phone, or other attributes change.

### Delete a contact

Delete a contact only when the record should be removed. Bulk delete actions may be available from the contact list.

## Contact details

Open a contact to review person-level context.

### Overview

The overview shows contact details and related activity.

### Conversations

Contact conversations show message threads linked to the person.

### Tickets

Contact tickets show follow-up work linked to the person.

## Filtering and views

Use search, filters, sorting, columns, and pagination to manage large contact lists.

### Search

Search helps find contacts by visible fields such as name, email, phone, or company.

### Bulk actions

Select multiple contacts when you need to perform list-level actions.
