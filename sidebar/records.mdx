Records keep customer context outside a single message.

## Overview

The Records section contains **Companies** and **Contacts**.

| Record type | Route | Shortcut | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Companies | `/{workspace}/companies/all` | | Account-level context. |
| Contacts | `/{workspace}/contacts/all` | `G` then `C` | People and their history. |

## Companies

Companies represent accounts or organizations.

### Company list

Use the company list to search, filter, sort, create, edit, and delete companies.

### Company details

A company can include overview details, conversations, tickets, contacts, peers, metrics, files, notes, and pricing.

### Shortcut

Use `G` then `C` to go to companies.

## Contacts

Contacts represent people connected to conversations and companies.

### Contact list

Use the contact list to search, filter, sort, create, edit, and delete contacts.

### Contact details

A contact page shows overview information plus related conversations and tickets.

### Shortcut

Use `G` then `C` to go to contacts.
